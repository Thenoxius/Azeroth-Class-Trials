# Class kit briefs (Hunter · Mage · Priest)

Read SPEC.md (contract, accuracy policy, ability tagging) and the research files first:
`research/forever-research.md`, `research/forever-facts.json`, and the raw sources in `research/raw/<class>-*.txt`.
The engine is in `src/engine/core.js` (read it — effects, auras, hooks, casting, pushback, FSR, threat) and `src/engine/ai.js` (pet behaviour).
The temporary `src/content/classes/mage.js` is a stub to be replaced; follow `src/content/common.js` for consumables and `spawnPlayer`.

## What a kit must deliver

- `export const KIT` per SPEC with `build({ level, spec, role, trialId })` for **levels 10, 30 and 60** (other levels may round to the nearest snapshot).
- **Toolkit breadth per snapshot** must follow research (abilities that exist at that level in WoW: Forever, or Classic baseline when unverified, labelled). Rough feel: L10 ≈ 4–7 buttons, L30 ≈ 9–14, L60 ≈ 13–20 (+ pet bar for Hunter). Never pad with invented spells. If Forever moved a key ability earlier/later (e.g. something that makes Shadow engaging earlier), reflect it and say so in `forever.note`.
- **Specs** at 30 and 60 (`KIT.specs[30]`, `KIT.specs[60]`): the real Forever specs/trees per research. At level 30 a "spec" means where the first ~21 talent points went. Differences between specs must be *mechanical* (different buttons, procs, loop), not just numbers. The player specifically asked about: Mage **Frostfire** builds (model it only if research shows such a build/talent exists in Forever; otherwise offer the closest real hybrid and label it), Priest **Shadow** vs a **healing** spec, Hunter pet-centric (BM) vs ranged (MM) vs whatever Forever's third tree is.
- **Roles:** Priest `build({ role:'healer' })` returns a healing kit (Holy/Discipline per research) for trials t3/t5; Shadow otherwise. Hunter and Mage are `['dps']`.
- **Hunter pet:** `pet:{ template, bar, autocast }` from the level the class gets a pet (Classic: taming at 10 — check Forever). Pet template uses `kind:'pet'`, `ai:{ type:'pet', rotation:[...] }`, focus resource if research says so, abilities like Bite/Claw/Growl/Dash per research. Growl autocast on by default (so dungeon players must notice it). Mend Pet / Revive Pet / Feign Death / pet commands must work through the engine.
- `loopHint` (1–2 sentences: the core loop at this level/spec — honest, e.g. "Apply Shadow Word: Pain, then wand until it falls off" at L10 if that's the truth), `newSince` (ids learned since the previous snapshot), `filler` (the ability the raid autopilot may cast), and consumables (default `['item.rest','item.healpot','item.manapot']`; Hunter may skip mana pots if no mana).
- **Every ability:** `name, icon (glyph id from SPEC list — pick the closest), school, mechanic, category, tags, learn, desc, forever:{status,note?,src?}` plus mechanics. Use `byLevel` for rank scaling. Projectiles for bolts/arrows (`projectile:{speed}`), channels for Arcane Missiles / Mind Flay / Mend Pet / Evocation-likes, `fx:'ring'` + `fxRadius` for self-centred AoE.
- **Authentic mechanics the engine supports — use them where real:** cast times and pushback; channel pushback; the five-second rule (`regen.spirit`, `castRegen` talents); Weakened Soul–style unique debuffs (`unique:'target'`); `frozen` auras + Shatter-style crit bonus via `critBonusFn`; `breakOnDamage` CC (Polymorph, Freezing Trap) and `breakDamage` fears; DR categories; `minRange` (Hunter dead zone if Forever keeps it); Auto Shot (`ranged.kind:'autoshot'`, `windup`, toggle ability with `toggle:'autoshot'`, shots with `startsAutoShot`); wand (`ranged.kind:'wand'`, toggle `'wand'`, `flags.wandMacro`); Feign Death (aura with `onApply` dropping threat via `api.dropThreat`, setting `u.flags.feigned`, `cancelOnMove`/`cancelOnAction`, `onRemove` clearing the flag); Fade (`threat` effect or temporary threat mod); Blink (`teleport mode:'blink'`); Ice Block-style `immune` aura; procs via `hooks` (Clearcasting, Spirit Tap, etc. — only if real in Forever/Classic and labelled).
- **Numbers are real, not tuned** (since 2026-10-27):
  - Spell and talent values: the datamined Forever ranks (research/forever-ranks.json, Wowhead spellbook/talent diff, live build 1.60.1.70630). For each snapshot use the highest rank learnable at that level. Spell power coefficients follow Classic rules (cast time / 3.5, DoTs duration / 15, below-level-20 penalty) unless the Forever tooltip states one.
  - Character stats: `src/content/stats.js` — Classic base stats per class, race and level (cmangos tables), a gear profile per level (our estimate: quest greens at 10 and 30, fresh dungeon blues at 60), self-buffs and group buffs by name, and the Classic formulas for health, mana, attack power, crit, dodge and spirit regen. No per-class damage multipliers.
  - Combat rules: `src/engine/combat.js` — armor, the Classic attack table (misses vs higher-level targets, dodge, parry from the front, glancing and crushing blows, crit suppression), spell hit by level difference and average partial resists.
  - Sanity bands, not targets (single-target dummy with real Classic armor, 120 s): L10 9–30 DPS, L30 45–110, L60 230–520. Measured on 2026-10-27: Hunters lead at 10 and 30 (pet + Auto Shot), casters lead on a dummy at 60; Hunters lead again in the raid because the boss's armor is debuffed and casters miss 17 % of spells against a level-63 boss.
  - Healer capacity: L30 ≈ 75 incoming DPS for 90 s; L60 ≈ 200 incoming DPS for 200 s (Holy more, Discipline less).

## Tests the kit agent must write

`tests/<class>-kit.test.mjs` using `tests/bots/harness.mjs` (`runTrial` + `dummyEncounter`):
1. Every level × spec × role builds; every bar ability resolves; every ability can be cast at least once on a dummy (or is legitimately situational — whitelist with a reason).
2. Dummy DPS (and HPS for healer on a "damaged ally" setup you build) per level/spec within the targets; print a table.
3. Sustain: a `brawler` dummy that hits back at each level — the bot survives and the resource curve looks sane (report time-to-OOM).
4. For Hunter: pet attacks, Growl taunts, Mend Pet heals, Feign Death drops threat and a lone mob evades when the pet is passive; dead zone behaviour if modelled.

And **bot policies** in `tests/bots/<class>.mjs` exporting `competent({ level, spec, role })` and `careless({ level, spec, role })`, each returning `(state, ctl) => void`. Competent bots must: pick/keep a sensible target (nearest engaged enemy; respect `state.script.focusId` if set), approach to range, run the real loop, use control when ≥ 2 enemies are on the player or a mob reaches melee (caster classes), use defensives below ~45 % HP, rest out of combat when HP/resource < 55 % and no enemy is engaged, step out of hostile zones (`ctl.inDanger()` → `ctl.escape()`), interrupt a casting target when the kit has an interrupt, dispel allies' dispellable debuffs when the kit can, manage the pet (attack command on target, passive when needed). Priest healer competent bot: triage by missing HP, keep tank alive, shield/renew/flash/greater-heal choices by deficit, dispel, mana awareness. Careless bots: only the filler on the current/nearest target, never move except to get in range, no control/defensives, no dispels, never rest. Trial authors will reuse and extend these.
